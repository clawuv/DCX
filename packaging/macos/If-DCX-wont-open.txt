If macOS won't open DCX
================================

DCX is free and open source, and it is distributed outside the
Mac App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it.

Option 1 - System Settings (easiest)
  1. Open DCX once. macOS shows a warning: close it.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down: next to "DCX was blocked", click "Open Anyway".
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. From now on it opens normally.

Option 2 - Terminal
  If macOS says the app "is damaged" or Option 1 isn't shown, open the
  Terminal app and run:

      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DCX.app

  Then open DCX again.

More help: https://github.com/adileo/dcx
